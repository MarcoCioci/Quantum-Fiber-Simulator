\chapter{Purpose of This Document}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Purpose of This Document}

This document collects detailed mathematical derivations supporting the theoretical and numerical development of the quantum fiber simulator.

The derivations reported here are not intended to interrupt the main thesis narrative. Their role is to provide a rigorous analytical reference for results that are used in the manuscript, in the MATLAB implementation, and in the validation of numerical experiments.

\medskip

The main thesis text contains the physical motivation, final mathematical results, and physical interpretation. Detailed algebraic passages, intermediate expansions, and reusable analytical validations are instead collected here.

\medskip

Whenever a derivation becomes essential for reader-facing reproducibility, it may later be adapted into the thesis appendices.
